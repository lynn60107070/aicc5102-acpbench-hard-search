\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We reproduced one task in full, plus the Progression task. Closed-model results could not be reproduced because the original API versions had been retired, and exact numbers are hard to replicate across hardware and precision. The original chat template and precision were not reported, and the 70B provider does not state its precision.

Our extensions cover two model sizes on one task with 130 test questions, so they may not generalise to other tasks or models, and the small test set limits statistical precision. Prompts were tuned on only 40 validation questions, and the budget limited inference and the number of experiments. The findings are evidence about the tested setup, not a general conclusion about search with language models.
