\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

We reproduced one task in full, plus the Progression task. Closed-model results could not be reproduced because the original API versions had been retired, and exact numbers are hard to replicate across hardware and precision. The original chat template and precision were not reported, and the 70B provider does not state its precision.

Our extensions cover two model sizes on one task with 130 test questions, so they may not generalise. With $n=130$, a 95\% binomial interval is about $\pm0.06$ at 0.16 and $\pm0.08$ at 0.36, and our per-seed $p$-values are uncorrected (with Holm correction, four of the twelve seed-pooled tests in Appendix~\ref{app:followups} survive). Ablations were run for 8B only. Under the frozen budget the search in effect reranks the root candidates, so deeper search is tested only in the 28-call ablation.  Design choices were made on 40 validation questions, too few to separate variants that differ by one or two questions (the chosen evaluator lost to fixed scoring on test), and the budget limited the number of experiments. The findings are evidence about the tested setup, not a general conclusion about search with language models.
