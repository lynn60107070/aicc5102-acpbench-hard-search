\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We reproduced the direct-generation baseline of ACPBench-Hard's Next Action task, matching four reported results within their 95\% intervals, with the 8B result confirmed by an independent re-run, and
asked whether LLM-guided search over LLM-predicted states improves on it. Under a matched call
budget it does not: search is lower than direct generation for Llama~3.1~8B and not significantly different for 70B. The search often proposes a correct action, but the model cannot reliably recognise it: even 70B misses it in 39\% of the questions where it is proposed. For open-ended planning without a
verifier, the evaluation of candidates, more than their generation, is the bottleneck. Pairing the same
search with a reliable external evaluator, learned or symbolic, is the natural next step.
