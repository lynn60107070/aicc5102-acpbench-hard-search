\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We reproduced the direct-generation baseline of ACPBench-Hard's Next Action task, matching four
reported results within their 95\% intervals and confirming them with an independent re-run, and
asked whether LLM-guided search over LLM-predicted states improves on it. Under a matched call
budget it does not: search is lower than direct generation for Llama~3.1~8B and on par for 70B. The
search often proposes a correct action, especially at 70B, but the model cannot reliably recognise
it, and a larger model does not judge its own candidates better. For open-ended planning without a
verifier, the evaluation of candidates, not their generation, is the bottleneck. Pairing the same
search with a reliable external evaluator, learned or symbolic, is the natural next step.
